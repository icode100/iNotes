iNotes for Apple silicon Mac

Drag iNotes.app onto the Applications shortcut, then launch iNotes from Applications.
Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.

This development build is signed locally. Public distribution requires an Apple
Developer ID signature and Apple notarization; a local signature does not establish
the publisher's identity. Review source and build instructions in the repository.

Keep your .inotes exports in a second location for backup. Google Drive storage
requires a Drive folder or a Files provider available on your device.

iNotes is open source under the MIT License. It is an independent application and
is not affiliated with Microsoft or Google. Microsoft OneNote files are not imported.
