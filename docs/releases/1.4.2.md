iNotes 1.4.2 / build 2024

- **Reveal page navigation at the bottom edge.** Move the pointer to the bottom of the window to see page numbers, **Browse pages**, and the **+** button. The bar overlays your writing and retracts when the pointer leaves it. On iPad, you can also tap the bottom handle.
- **Keep the page bar visible.** Click the pin on the bar, or choose **Appearance → Page navigation → Pin to bottom**. Click the pin again to return to **Show on hover**, the default. iNotes remembers your choice across windows and launches.

Both options work in an ordinary window, in iNotes’ **Full Page** view, and in macOS full screen using the green window button.
