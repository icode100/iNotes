iNotes 1.4.0 / build 2022

- **Turn pages like a notebook.** Use the page dots at the bottom to move through a topic. The adjacent **+** opens a blank page. Undo and Redo are available in the drawing toolbar.
- **Float the toolbar in full screen.** In **Appearance → Full-screen toolbar**, choose an always-visible bubble or a small handle that reveals it on hover. Drag the bubble to park it, then click it to open the tools in a circle. This works in Full Page and in a Mac window’s native full screen. Docked is the default.
- **Export a topic or workbook on Mac.** Save a topic or chapter as one PDF with a PDF page for each notebook page and bookmarks for its nested topics. To keep topics in separate PDFs, export a topic or an entire workbook as a ZIP. Choose the save location, and the bundle preserves the nested folder structure.
- **Build and edit diagrams on Mac.** Hover over a shape, table, arrow, image, positioned text box or equation and click **+** to place a connected shape or table. The new arrow follows its targets and switches to facing edges as you move them. Select an arrow to edit its route, heads, line pattern, width or color. You can also fill an existing shape and drag table dividers to resize rows or columns.
- **Change ink after writing.** Select handwriting on an editable Mac or iPad page and choose a new color. Undo restores its previous color. Under **Appearance → Notebook defaults…**, choose your starting pen widths and colors, paper and template for new pages.

iNotes opens archives from versions 1 through 5 of its file format. Custom table proportions, arrowhead styles and newer connector attachments use format 5. Open those notebooks with iNotes 1.4 or later. Pages containing diagrams or images open read-only on iPad.

On Mac, choose **iNotes → Check for Updates…** or download the Apple silicon installer from this release. The app uses ad hoc code signing and the existing Ed25519 update identity. Apple notarization is separate.
