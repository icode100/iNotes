iNotes 1.0.2 makes sheet navigation more flexible and gives each page a clear place in its notebook.

- Resize the sheets pane or show and hide it independently of the workbook sidebar.
- Sequential sheet numbers are assigned within each chapter or topic and appear with creation dates in the sheets list and page header. Numbers stay fixed through edits, Trash, and deletion; moving a sheet to another chapter or topic assigns its next available number.
- Sheet titles are optional, so a new page can remain untitled.
- The page header shows the nearest containing chapter or topic and can be hidden to leave more room for writing.
- A small sheet-number badge remains in the top-right of the workspace, including full-page mode and when the heading or drawing toolbar is hidden.
- Selected drawing tools use the workspace theme’s accent color.
- Unselected sheet cards use a subtle light/dark surface so titles, dates, previews, and breadcrumbs stay readable over scenic photographs.
- Photographic light/dark themes, shared text and handwriting, and the v0.2.2 drawing-cursor behavior are retained.

The Mac build requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. It is ad hoc signed and not Apple notarized; first launch of a downloaded copy may require System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
