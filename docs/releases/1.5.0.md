iNotes 1.5.0 / build 2026

Build 2026 removes the white line beneath the zoom slider in the bottom page bar and in Notebook defaults. Dragging adjusts zoom in whole percentages, and the percentage field accepts fractional values.

Choose **iNotes → Check for Updates…** or download the installer named `iNotes-1.5.0-build2026-arm64.dmg`. The original build 2025 installer is also available as `iNotes-1.5.0-arm64.dmg`.

**Also in iNotes 1.5.0**

- **Return to a page at the zoom you chose.** Each page remembers its zoom on this device, even after you quit iNotes. Use the slider in the bottom page bar, type a percentage from 25% to 400% (including values such as 87.5%), or choose **Fit Page** or **Fit Width**. Pages start at 100%. Change the starting zoom for pages without a saved choice under **Appearance → Notebook defaults…**.
- **Export a workbook as one PDF on Mac.** Right-click the workbook in the sidebar, choose **Export workbook as single PDF…**, and pick a save location. The workbook title appears on the cover. Each chapter starts with a numbered title page, and each topic and subtopic has a heading above its first note. Use the PDF bookmarks to jump between sections. **Export workbook as PDF bundle (ZIP)…** is still available for a folder of separate PDFs.
- **Leave Full Page from the floating toolbar.** Click the inward-pointing arrows to return to the regular workspace. The red, yellow, and green window controls stay visible when you use Full Page in an ordinary Mac window.
