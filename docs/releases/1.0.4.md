iNotes 1.0.4's backup-retention rebuild limits the accumulation of complete notebook copies.

- Automatic backups and **Back up now** keep the latest 5 copies, up to 7 daily and 4 weekly recovery points, targeting 500 MiB per local library and connected destination. A file can satisfy multiple retention categories.
- Identical copies are skipped. Cleanup verifies recovery copies first and protects the newest two valid backups. Unreadable or unremovable files are kept with a warning, so the budget can be exceeded. Independent exported backups remain permanent.
- **Automatic backups → [destination] → Review older backups…** shows existing backups for explicit selection and confirmed cleanup. The two newest valid older copies remain protected; large folders are handled in bounded batches.
- Frequent local autosave, your previous saved version, portable archive compatibility and independent cloud-folder backups continue. The provider handles upload/deletion synchronization; iNotes does not automatically merge concurrent edits.

The original v1.0.4 image and sidebar improvements remain included:

- Copy an image or image file, then press **⌘V** on the open sheet. Text and ink remain above the image on the same page.
- Use **Select** to move an image or drag its lower-right selection handle to resize it proportionally. Select an image and its annotations together to move or copy them as a group. Resizing an image alone leaves its annotations independent.
- Images are saved inside the notebook and included in combined PDF/PNG exports. Image insertion, movement, resizing and deletion support undo/redo. Rejected page changes retain recoverable history.
- Scenic sidebars use clearer labels and a stronger background surface for better readability.

PNG, TIFF, JPEG, HEIC, GIF (first frame) and BMP clipboard images are supported,
within the 10 MiB/image, 16-megapixel and 8192-pixel-per-side limits. The complete
notebook remains subject to the 64 MiB archive limit.

Adding the first image upgrades a notebook to archive format 3. Older iNotes
versions refuse that format rather than discard images. Existing format 1/2
notebooks remain readable; image-free notebooks do not upgrade unnecessarily.
Image authoring is Mac-only. The shared iPad source preserves image-bearing
sheets and marks them read-only with a notice until iPad image support is added.

Validation evidence and limits are recorded by build in `docs/TESTING.md`.
The publishing workflow runs core tests, Mac packaging, native regressions and
update-signature checks before publication. Source checks also compile the iPad
simulator target. These do not establish physical stylus or real cloud-delivery
coverage.

Use **iNotes → Check for Updates…** to install the newer build. The visible version
remains 1.0.4; Sparkle recognizes the higher build number. The original version
tag and original build-2012 installer are preserved. The app requires Apple silicon and macOS 14+.
It remains ad hoc signed and not Apple notarized. Existing Ed25519 signatures
authenticate the update feed and download; they do not replace notarization.
