iNotes 1.0.1 adds photographic workspace themes.

- Coast, Forest, and Alpine use the six supplied original photographs, with distinct light and dark images.
- Artwork follows the selected application appearance, including Match system.
- Contrast overlays and a soft fade keep headings and navigation readable, including Increased Contrast.
- Photos are bundled locally for Mac and iPad and shared through a small native-image cache. They add about 18.3 MiB to the app.
- Page colors, handwriting, typing, and the v0.2.2 cursor behavior are retained.

The Mac build requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. It is ad hoc signed and not Apple notarized; first launch of a downloaded copy may require System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
