Features carried in this unpublished source:

- **Matching paper:** page colors now follow the scenery and appearance, including maroon-black paper for dark Ruby Hills. Classic keeps its existing paper colors.
- **More papers:** Vintage White, Vintage Black, Pitch Black, Pitch White, and pastel Peach, Rose, Lavender, Mint and Sky. Explicit paper choices stay fixed across scenery and appearance changes; pitch papers use grey dots, rules and grids.
- **More ink colors:** sixteen named presets and a native custom color picker. Existing ink keeps its canonical saved colors; dark papers adapt their display for readability.
- **Blank pages first:** New sheet creates immediately without a naming dialog. Type into the underlined title area at the top to name the sheet; clearing it leaves the stored title blank. Sheet numbers and creation dates remain separate.
- **Page zoom:** Mac pages initially fit the visible editor width. Command-Plus (or Command-Equals) and Command-Minus zoom in 20% steps within 25–400%; View → Fit Page Width restores automatic fitting. Manual zoom survives theme and pane changes.
